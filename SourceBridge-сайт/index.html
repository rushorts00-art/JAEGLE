FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

What's the minimum order quantity?

Most categories work at 50–150 units with the factories we source from. Negotiating the MOQ down is part of the job — so you can test a product without burying yourself in inventory.

Do you mark up the product price?

No. You see the factory's own quotation and invoices. Our fee is a flat 8% of the order value — that's the only way we make money, and it's printed on every report.

How do payments work?

You can pay factories directly, or through our US entity via standard business channels — your choice. We never require 100% upfront: the standard is a 30% deposit and 70% after quality control passes.

How fast will goods arrive in the US?

Air freight runs 7–12 days door to door; sea freight 25–40 days. We handle formal customs entry for commercial batches and include a landed-cost estimate in every quote — no surprises at the border.

Can you ship straight to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We label and pack to Amazon's requirements, or deliver to your 3PL for final prep. FBA-bound shipments include the documentation checklist so nothing gets stuck at receiving.

What categories do you avoid?

For new clients we stay away from FDA-regulated goods, children's products, and certified electronics — compliance cost usually outweighs the margin. Almost everything else is fair game.
